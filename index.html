Signal rules

BUY candidate: EMA 9 above EMA 21, RSI between 52 and 68, and the latest close above EMA 9.

SELL candidate: EMA 9 below EMA 21, RSI between 32 and 48, and the latest close below EMA 9.

WAIT: conditions disagree, data is stale, or there are too few candles. A signal is a technical condition, not a prediction or guarantee.

Safety & data notes

This is not connected to your MT5 account and cannot see your balance or positions. Data can differ from your broker's XAUUSD feed. Confirm quotes, spread, session status and candle timing in MT5 before acting. No orders are placed by this app.

Free API quotas are limited. Fetch manually rather than refreshing repeatedly. If data is unavailable, check the provider's symbol access and plan.
